Gym Coach

45-minute full-body workout for two. Spoken guidance and timers.

The rules

  • Pick a weight where the last 2 reps feel hard but clean.
  • Start lighter than you think.
  • Sharp or joint pain means stop and use the swap.
  • Ask the attendant to set seat heights.

Turn volume up and switch off silent mode (iPhone side switch) so beeps play. Keep this page open; the screen will stay on.

Warm-up 5 min: 2 min easy bike or treadmill, 3 min mobility.
Main circuit, 3 sets each, side by side: leg press, lat pulldown, bicep curl, triceps pushdown, leg curl or extension, plank, bicycle crunch, chest press. Rest 45s between sets.
Him: 10-12 reps. Her: 12-15 reps.
Finisher 5 min: her incline walk, him bike intervals.
Cool-down 3 min: stretches.
